Global spec (applies to every video)

Technical - H.264, MP4 - 1080 x 1920 (9:16) - 60 fps

Screen recordings (used for all on-device actions) - iPhone with iOS 27 - For each logged recording, log in as Tweezy, with fresh settings (no subscriptions, no Alerts, all pages set to default) - When the entire iPhone screen is visible, place the recording in a frame of a real iPhone device on a dark background - Screen taps shown with an indicator added in post (same finger as in house banners), with a $green highlight on tap

Subtitles (all spoken text) - $white text on $blue2 background (or another color from the palette) - Verdana - ~24 characters per line, including spaces

Other rules - "iOS 27" label on the first few frames of every video (iOS changes; what we demo may not match other versions) - Videos no longer than ~90 seconds - Audio is recorded before screen recording (same as animated films) - Reduce production quality to optimize speed - Use a video editor aimed at average users, not professionals (e.g. CapCut or Canva) - Limit advanced, time-consuming effects and edits

Glossary - VN: Volatility Now (block at the top of the homepage) - PWA web app: Forex Factory added to the iPhone home screen - MV homepage: mobile-view homepage - LS: Layout Selector - TE: Trade Explorer - Showstory: the news story page

iPhone screen recording of the three Broker Guide tables, user scrolling through Featured Broker Guide for United Arab Emirates, opening details for a cell, closing the overlay and going back to Broker Guide.

EMILIANN

The Forex Factory Broker Guide is separated into up to three tables depending on your country: Domestically Licensed, Domestically Available, and Featured.

The Domestically Licensed table includes brokers that have a license from a regulatory authority that regulates brokers in the current country.

Such as DFSA or CMA in the United Arab Emirates.

The Domestically Available table includes brokers that operate in the current country, but don't have any domestic license.

And the Featured table can include brokers that are both domestically licensed or simply domestically-available.

On the Broker Guide you can compare brokers across a wide variety of criteria:

Founded date, domestic license, regulators, max leverage, live spreads, and many more.

Any box with a white triangle in the bottom right can be tapped to open up more details.

Once you find a broker you like, you can open an account by taping either the "Live" or "Demo" link.

Calendar

EMILIANN

You'll find the Calendar on the Forex Factory homepage, or in the dedicated Calendar tab.

It lists the day's scheduled economic events.

To find the next one, look for the green "Up Next" arrow.

Each row tells you when an event is happening, the currency it relates to, the expected impact, and the result once it is released.

Forex Factory's color rating shows the expected market impact: yellow is low, orange is medium, and red is high. That rating can change from one release to the next.

Tap an event to see the market's Forecast and the previous result.

For more context, open the folder for an explainer, event history, and any available previews or analysis.

And the graph makes it easy to see how the data has moved over time.

Want to stay on top of this event? Tap the bell for alerts now and on future releases.

As release time approaches, the countdown begins.

When the data is released, the Actual value appears. Its color shows how it compares with the Forecast.
