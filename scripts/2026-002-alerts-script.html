Shot list

1A · Intro, 360 fisheye phone POV Camera
  • 360 camera (e.g. Insta360 X series) propped on the desk where the phone sits, about her chest height, one lens facing her. She works at the desk until the buzz.
  • Use the 60 fps 360 mode. Export 9:16 with the fisheye view, widened until the black ring shows.
  • Keep her face near the centre of the circle; the edges stretch. Her hand reaching in will look huge, which is the point.
  • It sees almost the whole room: hide the crew and gear.
  • Stabilization removes real shakes, so add the shake in post: 2–3 short pulses timed to a buzz sound.
  • Add the Forex Factory alert banner at the top in post, bent to the fisheye curve and shaking with the image.
  • Turn off the "Shot on Insta360" watermark.
1B · Intro on camera Camera
  • Cam A handheld medium. She starts with her hand and the iPhone covering the lens, pulls it away to chest height, glances at the alert, looks to the lens and says the line.
  • She holds the real iPhone, not the 360 camera. The hand over the lens hides the swap.
  • Same hand and speed as 1A. Same sleeve, rings and nails.
  • Optional Cam B tight on the phone.
2 · Web app setup Screen
  • Before recording: remove other FF web apps and clear Safari history (Settings › Apps › Safari › Clear History and Website Data). Logged in.
  • Safari on the Forex Factory homepage → three dots → Share → View More → scroll a little → Add to Home Screen → Add → tap the FF icon.
3 · Calendar alerts Screen
  • Record this first on the fresh phone, so the iOS permission prompt appears in it.
  • Calendar for Sep 16, 2026 → scroll to USD Federal Funds Rate → tap the row → bell → Allow → Exit.
4 · Scanner alerts Screen
  • Homepage → tap the Scanner alerts bell → drag to the end of the Scanner.
5 · News alerts Screen
  • Forex Factory › /news → scroll so "News / Latest Stories" sits at the top (keeps the first inline ad out) → tap the bell.
6 · Outro Camera
  • Prop iPhone unlocked on the home screen with the FF web app, screen facing camera.
  • Phone time set to 1:59 pm; the alert arrives at 2:00. The clock won't trigger a real push: get a test push from the FF dev team, or add the banner in post.
  • Same framing as the intro so the video loops. Cam A handheld medium, Cam B close on the alert.

Editing the screen parts

  • Put the recording in a phone frame, a little smaller than full screen: label above, captions below, nothing over the buttons being tapped.
  • Keep the bottom 20% clear for the TikTok and Shorts buttons.
  • Every voice-over line also appears as a caption, so it works on mute.
  • Record the voice-over in the same room and mic, right after the camera takes.
  • Speed up the dead time between taps, never the taps. The setup is the longest part, so make it the fastest.
