## Global spec (applies to every video)

**Technical**
- H.264, MP4
- 1080 x 1920 (9:16)
- 60 fps

**Screen recordings** (used for all on-device actions)
- iPhone with iOS 27
- For each logged recording, log in as **Tweezy**, with fresh settings (no subscriptions, no Alerts, all pages set to default)
- When the entire iPhone screen is visible, place the recording in a frame of a real iPhone device on a dark background
- Screen taps shown with an indicator added in post (same finger as in house banners), with a $green highlight on tap

**Subtitles** (all spoken text)
- $white text on $blue2 background (or another color from the palette)
- Verdana
- ~24 characters per line, including spaces

**Other rules**
- "iOS 27" label on the first few frames of every video (iOS changes; what we demo may not match other versions)
- Videos no longer than ~90 seconds
- Audio is recorded before screen recording (same as animated films)
- Reduce production quality to optimize speed
- Use a video editor aimed at average users, not professionals (e.g. CapCut or Canva)
- Limit advanced, time-consuming effects and edits

**Glossary**
- **VN**: Volatility Now (block at the top of the homepage)
- **PWA web app**: Forex Factory added to the iPhone home screen
- **MV homepage**: mobile-view homepage
- **LS**: Layout Selector
- **TE**: Trade Explorer
- **Showstory**: the news story page
